# Human video style

Apply these rules after the canonical story is approved.

## Take selection

Rank technical quality separately from delivery quality.

The pipeline may measure:

- missing or clipped audio
- focus and exposure
- black or frozen frames
- transcript alignment
- repeated lines
- silence

The human decides:

- authenticity
- energy
- acceptable hesitation
- semantic emphasis
- which human imperfections remain

## Editing

- Preserve complete thoughts.
- Enter before the first necessary word, breath, or action.
- End after the payout and its natural breath.
- Propose removal of repetition, not conversational identity.
- Use J-cuts when voice continuity helps a proof cutaway.
- Keep edit handles in the EDL.
- Never silently repair a semantic change.

## Face and screen

- Use the face for trust and interpretation.
- Give proof the full canvas when it must be inspected.
- Avoid a tiny code surface behind a large face.
- Crop code to the active semantic region.
- Remove cursor wandering when it does not communicate intent.
- V1 uses an approved manual face crop.

## Captions

- Derive captions from corrected timed words.
- Preserve case, punctuation, product names, and code tokens.
- Prefer short semantic chunks.
- Use no more than two lines unless an accessibility review justifies it.
- Avoid covering the face, proof, or platform interface.
- Keep the captionless clean master.

Caption animation is a renderer decision. Caption timing and semantic grouping belong here.

## Subject masks

Subject masks are optional. When used:

- record the tool and parameters
- inspect hair, hands, glasses, chair gaps, and motion blur
- review first, middle, and last frames of every affected shot
- repair only failing ranges
- keep the original source untouched

Another person's appearance requires the consent state defined by the session contract.

## Contact sheet

Include:

- opening frame
- every major proof transition
- dense caption frames
- mask-risk frames
- final frame

The contact sheet is a review aid, not proof that the full video is correct.

## Acceptance

Reject or block when:

- a cut changes meaning
- a technical term is wrong
- proof cannot be inspected
- a face or private screen lacks review
- captions collide with core content
- a mask visibly fails
- voice is missing or clipped
- the output cannot be reproduced from sources and EDL
